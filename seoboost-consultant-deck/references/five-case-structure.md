# Five Case Structure & Slide Archetypes

The reusable backbone of a strategic business-case deck. Distilled from a real
infrastructure business-case presentation; all examples below are **generic** — replace
with the actual engagement's content.

## The narrative arc (section order)

| # | Section | Question it answers | Typical slides |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | **Cover + Disclaimer** | Who, what, for whom, when | Cover, disclaimer/limitations |
| **A** | **Project Overview** (exec summary) | What are we recommending, in one section? | Study components, timeline/status, stakeholder journey, demand summary, plan summary, business-case summary, recommended option |
| **B** | **Strategic Case** | *Should we do this at all?* | Strategic fit (3 lenses), context/connectivity, problem definition, enablers |
| **C** | **Demand / Evidence** | *Is there real need?* | Demand assessment per segment, capacity gap, catchment, sensitivity ranges |
| **D** | **Options / Phased Plan** | *What exactly, and in what sequence?* | Option long-list → filtering (MCA) → short-list, sizing (demand → phases → requirements), phase 1–N maps, land/asset |
| **E** | **Strategic Business Case** | Worth it? Structurable? Affordable? Deliverable? | Economic Case (GVA/benefits) · Management Case (institutional options) · Commercial Case (structure, revenue) · Financial Case (capex, opex, revenue build, IRR/NPV) |
| — | **Conclusion** | So what, now what | Conclusion, recommendation, next steps, thank-you |
| — | **Annex** | Backup / technical / regulatory | Technical assessments, regulatory eligibility, detailed tables |

> The **Five Case Model** proper = Strategic · Economic · Commercial · Financial ·
> Management. Sections B–E above map onto it; A is the exec summary that front-loads the
> answer (answer-first / Pyramid Principle).

## Slide archetypes

Pick the archetype that fits the idea; don't invent layouts per slide.

1. **Cover** — full-bleed hero image (top ~55%), navy band (bottom ~45%) with title +
   subtitle + "Final Presentation / date", partner logos, brand mark.
2. **Section divider** — navy full-bleed, big letter+title (`"B. Strategic Case"`),
   optional agenda rail highlighting current section.
3. **Action-title + body** — the workhorse. Navy action-title sentence at top; body =
   bullets, a 2×2, a framework, or a diagram. One idea.
4. **Data / chart** — action title states the takeaway; chart proves it. Every axis
   labelled, every number sourced via footnote.
5. **Options comparison** — table with options as columns, criteria as rows, RAG
   (red/amber/green) or ✓/✗ cells, recommended column highlighted.
6. **Phase / roadmap** — timeline or staged map (Phase 1 → N) with opening dates + what
   each phase adds.
7. **Financial summary** — capex/opex/revenue build + headline metric (IRR, NPV, payback)
   with the key assumptions stated on-slide.
8. **Conclusion / recommendation** — the single recommendation, the case for it in 3–5
   bullets, and next steps.

## Action-title discipline (the core craft)

| Label (weak) | Action title (strong) |
|---|---|
| "Demand Assessment" | "Without a second airport, the region runs out of capacity by 2030¹" |
| "Institutional Options" | "A public-commercial (PPP) structure best balances control and delivery risk" |
| "Financial Case" | "Phase 1 needs public funding; commercialisation from Phase 2 lifts IRR to ~X%¹" |

Rules:
- Every content slide has a **full-sentence** title with a verb and a so-what.
- Reading only the titles, top to bottom, must reproduce the argument.
- If a title could headline two different slides, it's too vague.

## QC checklist (before delivery)

- [ ] Titles-only read-through tells a coherent story
- [ ] Every content slide = one idea, action-title phrased
- [ ] Every number has a footnote (source · year · key assumption)
- [ ] Exec summary (Section A) front-loads the recommendation
- [ ] Options slide shows the criteria and why the recommended option wins
- [ ] Financial metric states its assumptions on the same slide
- [ ] Real brand palette/logo swapped in (not the placeholder navy/red)
- [ ] Disclaimer/limitations slide present for external distribution
